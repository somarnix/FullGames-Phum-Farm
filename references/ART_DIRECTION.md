# Starter Farm Commercial Art Target

The companion image `starter_farm_commercial_target.png` is the visual-quality target for the Level 1 farm. It is a reference image, not a texture or background to place behind the playable world.

## Required look

- Premium stylized 3D with optimized, richly detailed geometry
- Fixed elevated 45-degree isometric gameplay composition
- Warm Cambodian tropical sunrise with soft shadows and atmospheric depth
- Khmer-inspired raised timber architecture and red-orange clay tile roofs
- Layered grass, dirt, mud, reflective water, dense tropical planting, and handcrafted props
- Readable navigation paths and interaction space around every gameplay object
- No text, lettering, logos, signs with writing, HUD, or UI baked into world assets

## Starter-farm composition

- One Khmer stilt farmhouse and one small matching barn
- Four crop plots
- One chicken enclosure with two chickens
- One feed mill and one blank delivery board
- Lotus pond, irrigation ditch, bamboo fencing, paths, bridges, palms, banana plants, flowers, hay, baskets, tools, cart, jars, crates, and lanterns

## Asset review rule

Reject replacement models that read as primitive cubes, cylinders, sphere trees, flat-shaded greybox objects, generic Western farm architecture, or sparse prototype content. Models should preserve the gameplay footprint and scale defined by the Godot asset-slot system while approaching the material richness, silhouette quality, and density of the reference image.

The original image-generation specification was categorized as `stylized-concept` and generated with the built-in image generator. It requested one coherent commercial mobile-game screenshot with no interface or text.

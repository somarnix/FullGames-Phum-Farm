# Phum Farm Master Blueprint

## Vision

Phum Farm is a warm isometric 3D farming game set in a stylized Khmer inspired countryside. The player restores a family farm, grows crops, raises animals, produces goods, helps village neighbors, and gradually opens the river and surrounding land.

## Player promise

The player always has a clear useful action: plant, harvest, collect, produce, sell, improve, decorate, or explore. Progress should feel calm and rewarding. Waiting supports the loop but should not stop all play.

## First product

The first product is a small complete single-player game for Windows. Android follows after touch controls and performance pass. It has one polished farm, four crops, three animals, four production recipes, orders, ten levels, expansions, a tutorial, daily goals, achievements, and reliable local saves.

## Art direction

Use colorful low-poly 3D art, an isometric camera, readable silhouettes, warm sunlight, green fields, red-brown roofs, waterways, palms, rice fields, and village details. Khmer inspiration should come from landscape, materials, plants, architecture, clothing, props, music, and naming without copying a specific commercial game.

## Product boundaries

The first release does not require accounts, multiplayer, trading, leaderboards, live events, a large backend, hundreds of items, or premium monetization. These features may be added only after the local farming loop is fun and stable.

## Success criteria

A new player can understand the game without outside help, complete the first tutorial, play the farm-to-order loop for at least 30 minutes, save and reload safely, and see a clear next goal.

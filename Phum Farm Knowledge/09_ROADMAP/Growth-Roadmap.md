# Growth Roadmap

This roadmap describes stages. There is no fixed three-year completion promise. A Hay Day scale game can require many years and a full team.

## Stage 1 Simple complete game

Finish the 12 build gates. Release one polished farm with four crops, three animals, four products, orders, ten levels, goals, expansions, localization foundation, and local saves.

## Stage 2 Deeper farm

Add more crops, animals, machines, recipes, decorations, storage upgrades, land plots, NPC orders, quest chapters, achievements, weather, orchard trees, farm editing, and better art.

## Stage 3 Wider world

Add fishing, boat deliveries, harbor, mine, town services, festivals, NPC schedules, story chapters, collectibles, more districts, and streamed world content.

## Stage 4 Connected game

Add accounts and cloud save first. Then farm visits, friends, trading, neighborhoods, co-op tasks, notifications, leaderboards, moderation, and anti-cheat. Build server authority for transactions and timers before real trading.

## Stage 5 Live operations

Add seasonal events, remote configuration, content delivery, analytics with consent, experiments, support tools, admin dashboard, economy monitoring, regular content packs, and incident recovery.

## Stage 6 Large commercial scale

Expand toward hundreds of recipes, many buildings, large regions, ongoing stories, broad localization, cross-device play, social events, accessibility, customer support, and continuous balance work. Team size, art throughput, backend cost, and quality determine the timeline.

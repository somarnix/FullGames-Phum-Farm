## Expansions

Animal Meadow unlocks livestock space. Production Village unlocks machines. River Orchard opens later farming and fishing hooks.

## Definition checklist

Every content entry needs a stable id, localized display key, unlock level, costs, rewards, timing, icon, fallback color or model, category, and test coverage. Renaming display text must not change the stable id.

## Expansion packs

After release, add content in small themed packs. A pack includes its definitions, models, icons, localization, balance review, tests, and save compatibility. Do not add empty folders for future content.
